Example 1

User request:
Start at the first field of view, capture a 100 ms image, and wait for 3 seconds. On each callback move to the next field of view and repeat the same image and wait.

DSL response:
initialise
    move first_fov
    image exposure 100ms
    wait 3s

callback
    move next_fov
    image exposure 100ms
    wait 3s

finalise

Example 2

User request:
Move to the first field of view during setup. During each callback, take a 25 ms image at led wavelength 515nm. No finalisation action is required.

DSL response:
initialise
    move first_fov

callback
    image exposure 25ms led 515nm

finalise
